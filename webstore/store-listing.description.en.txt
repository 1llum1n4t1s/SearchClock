SearchClock is a Chrome extension that narrows Google search results to the period you choose.
A panel on the search results page lets you switch the period with one click, right there.
Turn on "Keep period" and the same period is applied automatically to your next searches too.

■ Features

・Panel next to the search results
  The period panel appears on the right side of the Google results page (at the bottom right of the screen on narrow windows).
  The results do not shift even if the panel appears late, which helps prevent mis-clicks.
  When you choose a period, the page switches right away to results limited to that period.

・Plenty of presets
  3 hr / 12 hr / 1 day / 3 days / 1 wk / 1 mo / 3 mo / 6 mo / 1 yr / 3 yr
  are available. Choose "Off" to clear the period on the page you are viewing.

・Two modes (the "Keep period" switch)
  OFF (once only, default): the chosen period applies to that search only, and the extension does not add a period to your next search automatically.
  This prevents searching with an unintended period because you forgot to reset the setting.
  ON (keep): the period selected when you turn the switch on is saved and applied automatically to your next searches too.
  The switch is at the bottom of the panel. It cannot be turned on while no period is chosen, or while Google is showing a period that is not one of the presets.

・Period added to the URL before the search
  While a period is kept, the extension uses Chrome's declarativeNetRequest to add the period to the URL at the search request stage.
  The results are never loaded first and then reloaded.

・Works with Google's search tools (last action wins)
  If you change the period in Google's search tools, "Keep period" is turned off automatically.
  If you choose a period in the panel, the page switches to that period. Other conditions, such as image size and sort order, are preserved.
  The most recent action always takes priority.

・Extension icon
  Click the icon to open Google in a new tab.
  While a period is kept, the badge shows its short form (1y / 1mo, etc.); in once-only mode it shows "·".

・Light/dark theme support
  The panel colors follow Google's light and dark themes.

・Lightweight and privacy-minded
  The only permissions used are declarativeNetRequest, storage, and access to the supported Google search domains.
  Your period settings are stored in Chrome and, if Chrome sync is enabled, are included in sync.
  No data is sent to any server of our own, and no external fonts are loaded.

・Supported Google search
  Works on the search results pages of 11 Google domains, including google.com and google.co.jp.

・Recommended for
  - People who want the latest information: find new information without being buried under old articles
  - Engineers and developers: programming information goes stale fast, so always search the newest articles
  - News and trends: people who want to narrow results to what is being talked about now

■ How to use

1. Install from the Chrome Web Store
2. Search on Google, and the SearchClock panel appears to the right of the results
3. Choose the period you want (for example: 1 yr) and the results are narrowed to that period
4. To use the same period for your next searches, turn on the "Keep period" switch
   (if it stays OFF, the chosen period applies to that search only)
5. If you change the period in Google's search tools, "Keep period" is turned off automatically
